Welcome, Hikers

Trail Finder is a user-friendly website that helps hikers find great local trails. Each trail has clear details, photos, and visitor ratings so you can choose a hike that fits you.

Perfect for beginner and casual hikers, families, and travelers looking for trails near their location.

What You Can Do

  • Search for trails by location and difficulty.
  • View trail length, photos, and directions.
  • Read visitor reviews and ratings.
  • Find trails near you with the locator.

How to Get Started

  1. Visit the Search page and choose your location and difficulty.
  2. Browse the Trails page to compare hikes.
  3. Use the Locator to see what is nearby.
  4. Contact us with feedback or trail suggestions.
